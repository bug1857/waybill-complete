# TSPLIB fixtures

Instances and optimal tours from TSPLIB95 (Gerhard Reinelt, Universität Heidelberg),
the standard benchmark library for the travelling salesman problem.

Retrieved 2026-08-29 from the coin-or/jorlib mirror of the TSPLIB95 archive:
<https://github.com/coin-or/jorlib> (`jorlib-core/src/test/resources/tspLib/tsp`),
except `berlin52.tsp`, retrieved from <https://github.com/mastqe/tsplib>.

| Instance | Nodes | Convention | Published optimum |
|---|---|---|---|
| eil51    |  51 | EUC_2D |    426 |
| berlin52 |  52 | EUC_2D |   7542 |
| st70     |  70 | EUC_2D |    675 |
| pr76     |  76 | EUC_2D | 108159 |
| kroA100  | 100 | EUC_2D |  21282 |

These files are external reference data. They are **not** generated by this project and
must not be edited. The published optima are cross-checked in `tests/test_tsplib.py` by
scoring the accompanying `.opt.tour` files with our own distance implementation — two
independent facts that must agree.
